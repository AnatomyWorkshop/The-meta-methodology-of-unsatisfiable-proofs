Illusion

Illusion is a structural search engine for proof barriers. Given a mathematical domain, it identifies discriminating transforms that separate provable from unprovable claims.

Validated across 6 domains:

Prism

Prism applies UCA spectral constraints to predict eigenvalue distributions of complex systems. Where random matrix theory gives universal statistics, Prism gives system-specific structural bounds.

Target applications: network risk propagation, materials phonon spectra, protein interaction networks, quantum hardware verification.

Status: undergoing external validation on public network datasets. We do not sell Prism until validation is complete.
